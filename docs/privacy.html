Vox Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Vox Reader reads web pages aloud with highlighting. Processing happens on your device. We do not collect, sell, or transmit your reading data to Vox Reader servers.

On-device data

  • Page text you choose to read
  • Preferences (speed, voice, colors) via Chrome Sync if enabled
  • Kokoro AI model cache in extension local storage

Network use

The extension may download the Kokoro model and voice files from Hugging Face when you enable or export with the AI voice. No page content is sent for cloud TTS.

Optional KReader sync

When enabled, the extension contacts 127.0.0.1:8766 on your computer only. Off by default.
