Vox Reader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · extension v2.6.5

Vox Reader reads web pages aloud with highlighting. Processing happens on your device.

What we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Vox Reader does not run analytics, advertising, or usage tracking.

On-device data

  • Page text you choose to read
  • Preferences (speed, voice, colors, shortcuts) via Chrome Sync if enabled
  • Kokoro AI model cache in extension local storage

Network use

The extension may download the Kokoro model and voice files from Hugging Face when you enable or export with the AI voice. Developer setup may also fetch the extension’s bundled dependencies. No page content is sent for cloud TTS, and Vox Reader has no reading or TTS servers.

Optional KReader sync

When enabled, the extension contacts 127.0.0.1:8766 on your computer only. Off by default.

  • Kokoro 82M (ONNX) — open-source neural TTS; model files are downloaded from Hugging Face.
  • ONNX Runtime Web — runs the model locally in your browser using WebAssembly.

Children

Vox Reader is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the extension evolves. Material changes will be reflected in the repository and extension listing.
